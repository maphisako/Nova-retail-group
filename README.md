PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project analyses retail data for Nova retail group to answer key business questions and generate data-driven insights and recommendations.

1.2.How many customers are in each region?

1.4.Which products have a unitprice of less than R1000?

1.5.How many feedback responses exist for each satisfaction level?

2.2.Which are the 5 top customers by total purchases?

2.3.What are the total sales by month for 2024?

2.4.Which channel performs better between Online and Store?

2.5.What is average customer rating for each product?

3.2.What is customer Lifetime value?

3.3.What is profit margin percentage for each product?

3.5.Which regions rank higher by total sales?
